PyroHorns is an AI assistant that runs on your own phone with local GGUF models.

<b>Current state (0.1.0, phase F1.1)</b>
<ul>
<li>Chat with simulated streaming replies; the real local model arrives in the next version.</li>
<li>Ten modes (general, planning, coding, debugging, research, Red/Blue Cyber…) that pick the right model.</li>
<li>Deep switch: collapsible reasoning and a larger context.</li>
<li>Conversations encrypted on the device (SQLCipher) with full-text search.</li>
<li>Backup password so your conversations can be restored from an Android backup.</li>
<li>Projects, GGUF model catalog and dark/light themes with the Plasma palette.</li>
</ul>

No ads, no telemetry. Open source under GPL-3.0.
